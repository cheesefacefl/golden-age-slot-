# 🎰 Golden Age Slots

A beautiful vintage-themed slot machine game with stunning golden-age aesthetics and smooth animations.

![Golden Age Slots](preview.png)

## ✨ Features

### Visual Design
- **Vintage Golden-Age Theme** - Rich wood-grain cabinet with brass/gold metallic accents
- **Beautiful Animations** - Smooth reel spinning with realistic deceleration
- **Elegant Typography** - Classic serif fonts with ornate styling
- **Responsive Design** - Works beautifully on desktop and mobile devices

### Gameplay
- **Classic 3-Reel Slot Machine** - Traditional slot machine mechanics
- **8 Unique Symbols** - Crown (👑), Seven (7️⃣), Diamond (💎), Bell (🔔), Star (⭐), Clover (🍀), Cherry (🍒)
- **Multiple Ways to Win** - Match 3 symbols or get 2 cherries for a payout
- **Adjustable Betting** - Bet from 10 to 50 credits per spin
- **Interactive Lever** - Pull the lever OR press the spin button
- **Keyboard Controls** - Spacebar to spin, arrow keys to adjust bet

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Open the Game** - Simply open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **Set Your Bet** - Use the + and - buttons to adjust your bet amount (10-50 credits)
3. **Spin the Reels**:
   - Click the red **SPIN** button
   - OR pull the **lever** on the right
   - OR press **Spacebar** on your keyboard
4. **Win Credits** - Match symbols on the center payline to win!
5. **Keep Playing** - You start with 100 credits. See how high you can go!

## 🎹 Keyboard Controls
